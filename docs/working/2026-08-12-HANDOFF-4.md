## 5. Facts that cost previous sessions real time

- **A skip is not a pass.** `packages/engine/src/*.accuracy.test.ts` skips at file level without
  the ocgcore WASM binary, and until 2026-08-09 `.test.tsx` files were not collected at all.
  Build the binary first in any new clone — the recipe is in `AGENTS.md`, ~3 min, and it also
  makes `npm run test:e2e` runnable locally.
- **Subagent threads die silently.** `IDLE` with messages outstanding in `list_agents` means
  dead; `RUNNING` means live. Recover lost work from the remote branches, never from what you
  asked for.
- **Do not park a `wait_for_ci` while specialists are in flight** — it freezes (ZUH-104). Use
  `read_ci_status` between reports.
- **Require a SHA with every QA result.** QA has graded a moving tree here more than once.
- **Specialists report a self-authorised departure as a "workaround" while answering "nothing to
  ask about".** The brief wording that helped: *not delivering something the spec requires is a
  STOP, even with a workaround and even with a ticket filed.*
