## Problem & users

**Users:** the founder + a handful of friends. A **closed, private, non-commercial** group — no public signup, no anonymous/ranked play, ever.

**Problem:** existing Yu-Gi-Oh apps are either polished-but-modern-only (Master Duel), or support the retro **Edison format** but wear dated UIs and/or don't enforce rules (Duelingbook is manual; EDOPro is accurate but a dated desktop client; Dueling Nexus is close but mid-2010s web and mobile-as-afterthought). **No app is polished + rules-enforcing + Edison + good on mobile at once.** That intersection is the wedge.

**Jobs to be done:** (a) build an Edison-legal deck; (b) duel a specific friend remotely; (c) learn/verify Edison rules so nobody is surprised by a judge at in-person tournaments. **Accuracy is the core promise** — see §5.

## Decisions made (CEO-confirmed 2026-07-13)

Decision records: `/mnt/memory/yugioh-app-team-memory/decisions/2026-07-13-*.md`.

1. **Reuse the community rules engine; accept AGPL-3.0.** Build on `ocgcore` (the **edo9300** fork — see §5, the MIT core is insufficient for era rules) + ProjectIgnis Lua CardScripts + `cards.cdb`; build a fresh web UI on top. Private/self-hosted; AGPL is a non-issue here and precludes only a future *closed-source commercial* product.
2. **Edison pool locked** to the **edisonformat.net** convention: legal cards through **Duelist Pack: Kaiba**; **The Shining Darkness and later EXCLUDED**; **March 2010 TCG Forbidden & Limited List** (**43 Forbidden / 70 Limited / 19 Semi**); plus a **27-card Duel Terminal 1 carve-out** (research resolved the "Hidden Arsenal" phrasing — see "Research closure" below). Legal pool ≈ **3,681 unique cards**. Mechanics: Synchro + Tuners, Fusion, Ritual, Gemini/Union/Spirit/Flip — **no Xyz/Pendulum/Link**.
3. **V1 enforces rules but does NOT explain illegality.** No in-duel "why is this greyed out?" tooltip, no disabled-action→rules deep-links, no reason-mapping layer (all V2, with the chatbot). **Explicit boundary — these STAY in V1** because they are enforcement / surfacing legal moves, not explaining illegality: (a) marking cards/actions with a legal action now; (b) **priority / quick-effect response windows** (engine pauses and offers legal responses only when you have one; auto-passes otherwise); (c) chain-stack visualization + step-by-step resolution; (d) deck-builder legality badges + blocking illegal decks.
4. **Architecture (native-server vs WASM) and card-data/image hosting are CTO-owned decisions.** This handoff states them as requirements-level constraints (e.g., "hidden info must never reach an unentitled client"), with research tradeoffs attached (§8), and does **not** pre-decide them.
5. **Per-duel auto-forfeit timer + asynchronous play.** Each duel carries an inviter-chosen **per-move timer** (presets 5 min/15 min/1 hr/12 hr/24 hr/48 hr; **48 hr hard ceiling, no unlimited**; bounded custom). Per-move deadline: the clock runs against whoever the engine is currently awaiting (incl. response/priority windows), resets when the awaited seat changes, counts **wall-clock time regardless of connection** (server-authoritative); expiry = **auto-forfeit, opponent wins (reason: timeout)**. This makes **asynchronous multi-day play first-class** (durable duels, resume across days) and **supersedes** the old "no auto-forfeit" disconnect default. **Notifications stay LIGHT in V1** (no push/email — friends nudge out-of-band); the in-app substitute is a **"Your move" queue** on home. Full spec: REQ-TIMER (below).

## Research closure (2026-07-13) — previously-open items now resolved

*Two research briefs closed the remaining desk-answerable questions. Detail: `docs/working/2026-07-13-research-edison-pool-exclusions.md` and `…-research-edison-functional-errata.md`.*

- **The in-window exclusion is Duel Terminal 1, not Hidden Arsenal.** The excluded cards are **27 Duel Terminal 1 (DT01, Jan 2010)** cards; **all 30 Hidden Arsenal 1 cards are LEGAL**. "Some Hidden Arsenal cards" was community shorthand. Mechanism: Konami policy — new Duel Terminal cards aren't tournament-legal until reprinted via a booster/deck, and these 27 were only reprinted in TSHD or later (out of window). Cross-verified two ways (edisonformat.net `EdisonCards.json` + the EDOPro Edison whitelist); authoritative list = edisonformat.com's "[E]-DT01 illegal" DuelingBook deck (id 8082483), passcodes in the research file.
- **Legal pool size ≈ 3,681** (edisonformat.net) / **≈ 3,671** (EDOPro whitelist). The community "~4,500" figure is wrong. **RESOLVED: follow edisonformat.net exactly** — the frozen allow-list = their `EdisonCards.json`; the ~12-card token/anime-promo delta is settled by matching their DB (tokens are engine-generated, not deck-buildable). Does NOT affect the 27-card carve-out.
- **Conventions confirmed:** Side deck **0–15** (not "exactly 15"). Who-goes-first = **winner of the toss chooses** play or draw. First player draws turn 1 **but cannot conduct a Battle Phase on turn 1** (add to the REQ-RULE QA checklist). Single games (no Bo3) confirmed by CEO.
- **Functional-errata list enumerated (feeds Spike B): 36 entries** (33 cards + 3 archetype reps). Breakdown: **6** have ready community pre-errata substitutes (Brionac→511002993, Sangan→511002631, Rescue Cat→511002992, Goyo→511002994, Brain Control→511002995, Future Fusion→511002997); **6 more** have Edison-correct scripts already in `pre-errata/` that the community banlist doesn't use (free accuracy we can claim: Necrovalley, Ryko, Catapult Turtle, Ancient Fairy Dragon, Darkness Approaches, Ultimate Offering); **~20** need per-card audit/authoring; **5** are rules-not-scripts (Lightsworn/Spirit phase-triggers, Union 1-per-monster, damage-step rulings) → handled by engine flags, not scripts. **Biggest gap: Red-Eyes Darkness Metal Dragon** — its only ProjectIgnis pre-errata script still enforces once-per-**name**; must be authored to per-copy. **Engineering lesson: the `pre-errata/` folder is NOT uniformly Edison-correct — inspect each `.lua`.**
- **Community-source corrections (fix in any earlier reference):** the working `!2010.3 Edison` **pool whitelist** is in **`ThaSMorato/alt-formarts-lflists`**; the **errata-substitution banlist** (the 6 substitutes) is in **`diamonddudetcg/edopro-custom-banlists` under its `Edison` git tag** (not `main`). The frozen allow-list **MUST accept the pre-errata alias passcodes** (e.g. Brionac `511002993`) or it will false-flag those staples.

## Requirements (summary; full testable set in the requirements doc)

113 requirements (91 MUST / 19 SHOULD / 3 COULD) across 13 areas, each uniquely ID'd and testable with edge cases attached. Highlights of the MUSTs by area:

- **Auth (REQ-AUTH):** invite-only account creation; no public signup/discovery; auth required for all surfaces; session persists and re-attaches to an in-progress duel; stable internal user id (display names may collide).
- **Lobby & challenge (REQ-LOBBY):** three primary actions (Duel a friend / Build a deck / Rules & rulings); presence + pending challenges; challenge→Accept/Decline; **atomic resolution of mutual/simultaneous/multi challenges** (no zombie challenges, no double rooms); shareable challenge link (members only).
- **Pre-duel room (REQ-ROOM):** both seats show ready-state + selected deck; a duel can't start until both are ready with **legal** decks; **who-goes-first by a method neither can rig**, shown to both.
- **Deck builder (REQ-DECK):** enforce the deck-construction contract exactly (Main 40–60, Extra 0–15 **Fusion+Synchro only**, Side 0–15, ≤3/name with banlist caps **Forbidden 0 / Limited 1 / Semi 2** across Main+Extra+Side combined); **alias/alt-art counts as the same card** (no copy-limit evasion); only frozen-allow-list cards addable; persistent legality badges; live counts + validity; illegal decks never usable in a duel; **`.ydk` import/export pinned exactly** (incl. the `!side` marker quirk, one passcode per copy, round-trip guarantee); import of foreign/illegal/malformed decks fails **informatively per-card/line**, never crashes or silently mutates.
- **Duel field & flow (REQ-DUEL):** render the Edison zone set only (no Extra Monster Zones/Pendulum/Link); always answer "whose turn/phase? / both LPs / what's it waiting on me for?"; persistent phase rail; every in-era summon type when the engine offers it; render engine sub-selections (tributes, Synchro/Fusion materials, targets, positions); Battle Phase interactions; discard-to-6; game-end detection incl. LP-0, **deck-out (lose on failing a required draw, not on empty deck)**, Exodia/effect win, simultaneous = **draw**, surrender.
- **Chains/priority (REQ-CHAIN):** response prompt **only when the engine says the player has a legal response**, else auto-pass; explicit Respond/Pass (no accidental single-tap pass); chain stack with owner + order; step-by-step top-down resolution; **Edison ignition-effect priority window** surfaced; SEGOC ordering choices rendered.
- **Engine rule-correctness (REQ-RULE):** engine configured to the **March 2010 / Master-Rule-1 era** (see §5) with the frozen pool + Edison `lflist.conf`; a QA checklist verifies ignition priority, first-turn draw, damage-step restriction, single-field-spell, exact-Level Synchro, **no Battle Phase on turn 1**, and duel constants in **live duels**.
- **Networking & hidden info (REQ-NET):** **hidden information (opponent hand/deck/order/set cards/face-down monsters) MUST never reach a client not entitled to see it**, regardless of architecture; authoritative state where the opponent can't read it; **no external ruling calls mid-duel** (engine + data self-hosted); disconnect preserves state and stays redacted; reconnect restores the correct redacted state with **seat integrity** (no seat hijack).
- **Rules reference (REQ-REF):** standalone, searchable, Edison-era content, self-hosted, mobile-usable.
- **Log & summary (REQ-LOG):** chronological action log **respecting hidden info** (no retroactive leak); scrollable; post-duel summary with winner/loser/draw + reason.
- **Responsive/accessibility (REQ-UX):** usable phone-portrait→desktop on one layout system; **every duel action completable by tap, drag never required**; phone "your-field-first" board (no zoom-to-act); reading a card always available and separate from acting; ≥44px targets / ≥16px text; **no meaning by color alone**; reduced-motion; dark theme.
- **Data (REQ-DATA):** Edison pool is a **frozen passcode allow-list = edisonformat.net's `EdisonCards.json`** (must accept pre-errata alias passcodes); metadata + images **self-hosted** (no hotlinking); banlist via Edison `lflist.conf`; no post-cutoff or Xyz/Pendulum/Link cards; builder display data and engine `cards.cdb` reconciled on identity/legality; **durable storage of in-progress duel state (REQ-DATA-06)** for async resume.
- **Per-duel timer & async (REQ-TIMER, new — 11 reqs):** one inviter-set **per-move timer** (presets to a **48 hr ceiling**, no unlimited, bounded custom, out-of-range rejected server-side), **fixed for the duel**; per-move deadline vs the awaited seat incl. response windows, resets on seat change, **counts wall-clock time regardless of connection** (server-authoritative), **expiry → auto-forfeit/opponent wins/reason=timeout**; **durable async persistence + resume across days** (survives both offline and server restart); **"Your move" queue on home (MUST)** + "waiting on opponent" list (SHOULD); timer shown to both in pre-duel room and on the field; **invitee sees it before Accept**.

## Engine & accuracy — R1 RESOLVED (the linchpin)

*This is the founder's #1 requirement, so it gets its own section. Full evidence (with engine file:line citations) in `/workspace/product/research/engine-edison-rules.md`.*

**Verdict: the engine CAN reproduce accurate Edison — this is NOT "2010 banlist on modern mechanics."** The **edo9300** `ygopro-core` fork was deliberately built with granular, individually-settable era-rule flags. Use the edo9300 fork (**the old Fluorohydride MIT core lacks these flags** — this removes any "use MIT to dodge AGPL" option; AGPL stands).

Set the duel-rule bitmask **directly in `OCG_CreateDuel(duelFlags)` server-authoritatively — the banlist file carries NO rule directive.** Recommended Edison flag set (verified against engine source; full rationale in §6 of the R1 brief):
- `DUEL_TCG_FAST_EFFECT_IGNITION` (+ `DUEL_OCG_OBSOLETE_IGNITION`) — **ignition-effect priority**, extended to GY effects (TCG-accurate; use the TCG flag, not just OCG). ✅ out of the box.
- `DUEL_1ST_TURN_DRAW` — first player draws turn 1. ✅
- `DUEL_1_FACEUP_FIELD` — single shared Field Spell, new one destroys old. ✅
- `DUEL_6_STEP_BATLLE_STEP` + `DUEL_SINGLE_CHAIN_IN_DAMAGE_SUBSTEP` + `DUEL_0_ATK_DESTROYED` — pre-2014 damage step. 🟡 **flags exist but only in the GOAT preset — compose a custom set; validate against 2010 rulings (Spike A).**
- `DUEL_TCG_SEGOC_NONPUBLIC` + `DUEL_TCG_SEGOC_FIRSTTRIGGER` — Edison SEGOC handling.
- Forbidden-types mask = Xyz | Pendulum | Link.

**Residual risk — card-level errata (MEDIUM, mitigable, a required workstream):** the core rules flag cleanly, but ProjectIgnis card **scripts ship MODERN errata**. Verified example: the shipped Brionac script has a hard once-per-turn (`SetCountLimit(1,id)`); **Edison Brionac has no once-per-turn** — a meta-defining discrepancy. ~3 dozen cards are on the Edison "Functional Errata" list (Brionac, Sangan, Ryko, Treeborn Frog, Black Garden, Goyo, REDMD, Prisma, …). **Required work:** curate a **pre-errata card set** — forbid modern errata passcodes and substitute pre-errata/alias passcodes, pull existing `pre-errata/`+`goat/` scripts, author the missing ones, and **maintain a documented gap list**. Research has now **enumerated the full list (36 entries) and the substitution mapping** — see "Research closure" above (6 community substitutes ready; 6 more existing scripts unused; ~20 to audit/author; biggest gap = **Red-Eyes Darkness Metal Dragon**, whose shipped pre-errata script is still wrong). Correct source repos: **`ThaSMorato/alt-formarts-lflists`** (pool whitelist) + **`diamonddudetcg`** under its `Edison` git tag (errata substitutions); and note the `pre-errata/` scripts are **not uniformly Edison-correct** — inspect each. Precedent: **Dueling Nexus already ships "Master Rule 1 + March 2010 banlist + pre-errata cards"** — this is solved-in-principle; our work is curation, not invention. Net: we can be *as accurate as or better than* any automated sim, with a small, documented residual — state that honestly rather than claiming perfection.

## UX flows & screens

Full wireframes (desktop + mobile) and the flow map are in the UX flows doc. **13 screens:** Login · Home/Lobby · Challenge-a-friend · Pre-Duel Room · Duel Field (desktop) · Duel Field (mobile) · Priority/Response + Chain + Resolution · Card Inspector · Deck Builder (desktop) · Deck Builder (mobile) · My Decks · Duel Summary/Replay · Settings/Profile · Rules & Rulings.

Key UX principles the build must honor: **surface legal actions** (actionable marker); the board answers three questions at a glance; **one interaction grammar across devices** (tap→action-sheet→pulsing-destination; tap-art/long-press to inspect, separate from acting); the **mobile 2-player board = "your-field-first" portrait** (your half full-size, opponent a tappable status strip — never cram both fields); **priority/chain is the make-or-break screen** — slow down, show the stack and top-down resolution with neutral event captions; **all narration is event-neutral** (states what happened, never a why-judgment) to keep V1 inside the no-explanation boundary.

**Timer & async UX (new):** the challenge flow gains a **"time per move" picker**; the invitee sees it before accepting and the pre-duel room shows it; the duel field shows a **countdown for whoever is on the clock** (in the turn/phase ribbon; adaptive granularity — days/hours far out, mm:ss only in the final minutes; muted on the opponent's clock, emphasized when it's yours and low). The home screen gains a **"Your move" queue** — the async in-app substitute for notifications: durable in-progress duels awaiting this player, **sorted by least-time-remaining first** with a ⚠ on near-expiry and a humanized "23h left" readout, each with **[Resume]** straight into the live field. Summary shows a **timeout/auto-forfeit** end reason.

## Recommended spikes before full build (priority order)

- **Spike 0 — licensing sign-off:** confirm AGPL + private-only (done at decision level; record stands).
- **Spike A — Edison ruleset validation:** stand up edo9300 core with the §5 `duelFlags`, run known Edison rulings (ignition priority incl. GY effects, damage-step cases, field spell, first-turn draw); lock the flag set. *Gates the accuracy promise.*
- **Spike B — pre-errata dataset build:** frozen passcode allow-list (accepting alias passcodes) + Edison `lflist.conf` + pre-errata script/db substitution; verify the 36 enumerated functional-errata entries (6 substitutes ready, 6 existing scripts to wire up, ~20 to audit/author, and **author Red-Eyes Darkness Metal Dragon** whose shipped pre-errata script is still wrong); produce the documented gap list.
- **Spike C — two-client relay with hidden-info redaction:** one authoritative core driving two browser clients with correctly redacted per-seat views (+ reconnect).
- **Spike D — durable async duel persistence & resume:** an in-progress duel survives **both players offline for days and a server restart**, and resumes to the correct state with the correct remaining move-clock (recommended approach: persist the deterministic response-log and replay to rebuild engine state — the same mechanism that powers replays). De-risks R11 and the timer/async feature.
