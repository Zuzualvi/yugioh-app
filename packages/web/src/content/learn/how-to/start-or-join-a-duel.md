> **Goal:** Create an invite-to-play link by picking a deck and a time-per-move, share it in your group chat, and join from a link to begin the duel.

Duels are **link-first**: you create an invite, drop it in your group chat, and whoever grabs it first becomes your opponent. There is no matchmaking and no "who's online" list — the link is the invitation.

## Create an invite to play {#create}

1. From **Home**, tap **⚔ Duel a friend**.
2. **Step 1 · Your deck** — pick one of your saved decks. (Build one first if the list is empty — see _Build & manage decks_.)
3. **Step 2 · Time per move** — choose how long each player gets for **each** move: **5 min · 15 min · 1 hr · 12 hr · 24 hr · 48 hr**, or **Custom**. Short = play live now; long = play out over days. The default is **24 hr**.
4. Tap **Create duel & get link ▸**.

## Share the link {#share}

1. On the **Duel Created!** screen, tap **🔗 Copy link** and paste it into your group chat, or share it however you like.
2. The link works for **one** duel with the first member who opens it.
3. Tap **Enter duel ⚔** whenever you want to wait at the board — the game begins the moment your opponent joins. For a slow (async) game you can leave and come back later; your per-move clock keeps the duel alive.

## Join from a link {#join}

1. Open the invite link a friend shared. You will see **"You've been challenged!"** along with the **time-per-move** the game uses.
2. Pick the deck you want to play and tap **Accept & enter duel ▸**.
3. You go straight to the board and the duel starts. (If the link says it has already started or expired, ask for a fresh one.)

## Who goes first {#who-goes-first}

Once both players are in, the duel begins right away — there is no separate ready-up room. The app decides who takes the **first turn**, and the **turn ribbon** on the board always shows whose turn it is. In Edison, the player who goes first **draws** for their turn and **cannot attack** on it.

**See also:** The first-turn draw and no-turn-1 attack rule is [Rule #1 · Starting player draws](/learn/rules/difference-01-starting-player-draws); the phase order is in [How a turn works](/learn/rules/primer-how-a-turn-works). Next, learn to [play a turn](/learn/how-to/play-a-turn).
