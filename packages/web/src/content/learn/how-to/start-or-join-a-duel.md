> **Goal:** Create a challenge link, share it with your opponent, meet them in the duel room, pick your decks, and start playing.

Duels are **link-first**: you create an invite, drop it in your group chat, and whoever opens it becomes your opponent. There is no matchmaking and no "who's online" list — the link is the invitation.

## Create a challenge link {#create}

1. From **Home**, tap **⚔ Duel a friend**.
2. **Time per move** — choose how long each player gets for **each** move: **3 min · 5 min · 10 min · 15 min**. The default is **10 min**. All options are for live play — you and your opponent play at the same time.
3. Tap **Create challenge link ▸**.

You pick your deck in the room, once your opponent has arrived.

## Share the link {#share}

1. On the duel room screen, tap **↗ Share link** to open your device's share sheet, or **🔗 Copy** to copy the link to your clipboard.
2. Paste it into your group chat or send it however you like.
3. The link works for **one opponent** — the first member who opens it joins your room.
4. **The link expires in 30 minutes.** If nobody joins in time, create a fresh one.

You can close the page while you wait; the link keeps working. Come back to the room to see when your opponent arrives.

## Join from a link {#join}

1. Open the invite link your friend shared. You will land in the **duel room** and see who sent it and what time-per-move setting they chose.
2. **Pick a deck** from the list, then tap **Ready ✓**.
3. You can leave any time before you ready — nothing is recorded and it doesn't count as a loss.

## The duel room {#room}

Both players meet in the room before the duel begins. The room has a few jobs:

- **Pick your deck.** Each player picks from their saved decks. The opponent never sees which deck you chose — only that you've chosen one.
- **Ready up.** Once you're happy with your deck, tap **Ready ✓**. Your deck is locked in. You can un-ready and change your deck until both players have readied.
- **Wait for each other.** The room shows each player's state — picking a deck, deck chosen, or READY. Both players must ready before anything happens.

The room link expires **30 minutes** after it was created. If both players are in but haven't readied in time, the room closes and a new challenge needs to be created.

## The coin flip {#coin-flip}

The moment both players ready, the app flips a coin. The winner of the flip **chooses who goes first** — themselves or their opponent. Once they choose, the duel begins.

In Edison format, the player who goes first draws for their turn and **cannot attack** on it.

## Who goes first {#who-goes-first}

The flip winner chooses. The **turn ribbon** on the board always shows whose turn it is.
