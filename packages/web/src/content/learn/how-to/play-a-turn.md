> **Goal:** Take your turn by tapping an action, choosing where it goes, and moving through the phases — and respond safely when the app opens a priority window.

You never have to remember what is legal. The app only ever offers you moves you are actually allowed to make, and the game only changes when you tap a labelled action.

## When it is your move {#your-move}

When the engine needs a choice from you, an **action panel** appears — a sheet that slides up from the bottom on phone and tablet, or a menu on desktop. It lists your legal options; the board stays visible behind it. If there is nothing to decide, the panel simply waits.

_[Screenshot: action sheet listing legal actions grouped by card]_

## Tap, choose, then pick a destination {#tap-choose-destination}

Making a play is a short, guided chain:

1. **Tap** — pick the card and action you want. Actions are grouped by card, e.g. **▶ Card — Normal Summon**, **Set (face-down)**, **Change Position**, or **Activate — <effect>**.
2. **Choose** — if the play needs a **destination or target** (a zone, face-up vs. face-down, a card to target or tribute), the app asks you in a follow-up prompt.
3. **Confirm** — once you have answered every prompt, the move happens and the board updates.

Only labelled action buttons commit a move. Backing out of a prompt leaves the game exactly as it was.

## Moving through the phases and ending your turn {#phases-end-turn}

When you have no more plays to make, use the phase buttons in the action panel:

1. **⚔ Battle Phase** — enter combat to declare attacks. (Not available on the very first turn of the duel.)
2. **⏹ End Phase** — finish your turn and pass play to your opponent.

The **phase rail** on the board (Draw · Standby · Main 1 · Battle · Main 2 · End) shows where you are. Ending the End Phase hands the turn over.

## Read a card without playing it {#inspect-safely}

Opening a card to read its art and details **never** triggers its effect or commits a play. Nothing happens to the game until you tap a named action button (**▶ …**). If you are just checking what a card does, read it and then **dismiss** the sheet — the board is untouched.

## Responding when the app offers a window {#responding}

Sometimes the app opens a **⚡ Priority window** — your chance to respond before something resolves (for example, before an opponent's card resolves, or after a summon):

1. Tap **Respond ▸** to see the effects you can activate, then pick one.
2. Tap **⬜ Pass** if you do not want to respond, and play continues.
3. If a response is required, the window will not offer Pass until you have answered.

You can leave a duel with **⚑ Resign** at any time from the action panel.
