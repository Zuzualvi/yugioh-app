> **Goal:** Sign in with your club invite and find your way around the Home screen — where duels, decks, and guides live.

Edison Duel is a private club, so there is no public sign-up. You get in with an **invite link** from a club admin, then everything starts from the **Home** screen.

## Sign in with your invite {#sign-in}

1. Open the **invite link** an admin sent you. It takes you to a **Set up your account** screen.
2. Enter a **Display name** (what other members see), **choose a password**, and confirm it. Tap **Sign in / create**.
3. You land on **Home**, signed in. Next time, open the app and use **Sign in** with the same display name and password.

If you open a duel invite while signed out, sign in first — the app remembers the link and drops you back onto it once you are in.

## The Home screen {#home}

Home is your hub. The top bar shows the **⟡ EDISON DUEL** wordmark, your name (**● Your name**), and **Sign out**. Below that, a **"What do you want to do?"** grid holds three cards.

## Where duels, decks, and guides live {#where-things-live}

1. **⚔ Duel a friend** — start or accept a game. Opens the create-a-duel flow. See _Start or join a duel_.
2. **🂡 Build a deck** — your saved decks and the deck builder. See _Build & manage decks_.
3. **📖 Rules & Guides** — this section: how-to guides plus the full Edison format rules, priority, the Damage Step, and card references.

Admins also see an **Invite a friend** button on Home for adding new club members.
