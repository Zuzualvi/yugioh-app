# Getting Started {#top}

> **PLACEHOLDER:** This page will be authored by Track C with verified content once the in-app docs surface (Track B4) ships. The structure below demonstrates the shell.

## Creating Your Account {#create-account}

You join the group via an invite link from an admin. Click the link, choose a display name, and set a password. Your account is ready immediately.

## Logging In {#login}

Go to the app URL and enter your display name and password. The app keeps you logged in for 30 days.

## What You Can Do {#what-you-can-do}

From **Home** you have three options:

- **Duel a friend** — challenge another group member to a real-time duel
- **Build a deck** — create and save Edison-format decks
- **Rules & Guides** — this documentation section
