**TL;DR:** Players cannot respond to the end-of-turn hand-size discard, but a mandatory Trigger Effect that the discard sets off builds a chain that only negation (Spell Speed 2) or Counter Traps (Spell Speed 3) may join.

At the End Phase you discard down to the **6-card hand-size limit.** In Edison this discard is a special, closed action — you can't respond to it — with one exception for mandatory triggers.

## You cannot respond to the discard {#no-response}

The end-of-turn hand-size discard must be the **last action of the turn**, and players **cannot respond to it at all.** There is no response window around the discard itself — no chaining Quick-Play Spells or Traps "to the discard."

## When the discard triggers a mandatory effect {#mandatory-trigger-chain}

If a **mandatory Trigger Effect** is set off _by_ the discard (a card whose trigger is "when this card is sent to the Graveyard" and similar), that trigger builds a chain with special, restricted properties:

- **Spell Speed 2** effects may be chained to it **only if they negate** activations or effects. A Spell Speed 2 effect that does not negate cannot join this chain.
- **Spell Speed 3** effects (Counter Traps) may be chained **regardless** of whether they negate.

**Example (mandatory trigger) —** You discard **White Stone of Legend** for hand size, and its mandatory Trigger Effect must activate. On the resulting chain:

- You **cannot** chain **Super Rejuvenation** (a non-negating Spell Speed 2 Spell) to it.
- Your opponent **can** chain a Set **Divine Wrath** (a Spell Speed 3 Counter Trap that negates the activation).
- A face-up **Doomcaliber Knight** immediately tributes itself and **adds a chain link**, because its Quick Effect is mandatory.

## Optional triggers cannot fire off the discard {#optional-triggers-cant-fire}

If a discarded card has an **optional** Trigger Effect ("you can" rather than "you must"), that optional effect **cannot be activated** when the card is discarded for hand size — the closed nature of the discard denies the optional window.

**Example —** You discard **Peten the Dark Clown** and **Red-Eyes Wyvern** for hand size. Neither card's **optional** Trigger Effect may activate — you cannot use Peten's Special Summon effect or Red-Eyes Wyvern's End-Phase effect off a hand-size discard.

## Related cards {#related-cards}

The mandatory-trigger example is **White Stone of Legend**, with **Super Rejuvenation** (a non-negating Spell Speed 2 that is locked out), **Divine Wrath** (a Counter Trap that may join), and **Doomcaliber Knight** (a mandatory Quick Effect that adds a link). The optional-trigger cards that are shut out are **Peten the Dark Clown** and **Red-Eyes Wyvern**.
