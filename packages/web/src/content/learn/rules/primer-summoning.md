**TL;DR:** You may **Normal Summon or Set one monster per turn** (higher-Level monsters cost Tributes). Everything else — **Flip Summon**, and the **Special Summons** (Synchro, Fusion, Ritual, and card-effect summons) — has no per-turn cap beyond each card's own conditions. This era's Extra Deck holds **only Fusion and Synchro Monsters**.

## Normal Summon and Tribute Summon {#normal-summon}

A Normal Summon plays a monster from your hand onto the field in **face-up Attack Position**. You may **Normal Summon or Set only once per turn** (in either Main Phase). Higher-Level monsters require you to Tribute (send to the Graveyard) monsters you control first:

- **Level 1–4** — no Tribute.
- **Level 5–6** — Tribute **1** monster.
- **Level 7 or higher** — Tribute **2** monsters.

A Normal Summon that needs Tributes is called a **Tribute Summon**. It still uses up your one Normal-Summon-or-Set for the turn.

> **Example.** You Tribute one monster you control to Normal Summon a Level 6 monster. That is your Normal Summon for the turn.

## Set {#set}

To **Set** a monster is to play it from your hand in **face-down Defense Position**. A Set monster is **not** considered Summoned yet — it is hidden, and its effects generally do nothing until it is face-up. Setting a Level 5+ monster still costs the appropriate Tributes, and Setting uses your one Normal-Summon-or-Set for the turn.

## Flip Summon {#flip-summon}

A **Flip Summon** turns one of your face-down Defense Position monsters face-up into **Attack Position**, without using a card effect. You can Flip Summon as many monsters as you like per turn, but **not** a monster on the same turn you Set it. Flipping a monster face-up activates any Flip Effect it has.

## Special Summon {#special-summon}

A **Special Summon** puts a monster on the field **without** using your Normal Summon or Set. It happens through a card's effect, or through a monster's own Summoning condition (for example, Cyber Dragon Special Summons itself when your opponent controls a monster and you do not). Special Summoned monsters enter in your choice of face-up Attack Position or face-up Defense Position, and you may perform as many as the cards allow.

Synchro, Fusion, and Ritual Summons (below) are all Special Summons.

After a Special Summon that does **not** start a chain, the turn player can act before the opponent responds — this is Edison's Ignition Effect Priority. See [Rule #6 — Ignition Effect Priority](/learn/rules/difference-06-ignition-effect-priority).

## Synchro Summon (and Tuners) {#synchro-summon}

Synchro Summoning is the signature mechanic of this era. You Special Summon a Synchro Monster **from the Extra Deck** by sending, from your side of the field to the Graveyard, **1 face-up Tuner monster plus one or more face-up non-Tuner monsters** whose **Levels add up to exactly** the Synchro Monster's Level. A **Tuner** is any monster with "Tuner" printed in its type line. There is no Rank or Xyz mechanic in this era.

> **Example.** You control Junk Synchron (a Level 3 Tuner) and Speed Warrior (Level 2). 3 + 2 = 5, so you send both to the Graveyard and Synchro Summon a Level 5 Synchro Monster from your Extra Deck.

## Fusion Summon {#fusion-summon}

A **Fusion Summon** brings out a Fusion Monster from the Extra Deck. Normally you activate **Polymerization**, send the specific **Fusion Material Monsters** listed on the Fusion Monster (from your hand or field) to the Graveyard, and Special Summon the Fusion Monster. Some cards perform a Fusion Summon with their own effect instead of Polymerization.

## Ritual Summon {#ritual-summon}

A **Ritual Summon** uses a **Ritual Spell Card** together with the matching **Ritual Monster** (which lives in your Main Deck, not the Extra Deck). You activate the Ritual Spell and **Tribute monsters whose Levels meet the requirement** printed on the Ritual Spell, then Special Summon the Ritual Monster.

> **Example.** You activate the Ritual Spell "End of the World," Tribute monsters totalling the required Levels, and Ritual Summon Demise, King of Armageddon.

## The Extra Deck: Fusion and Synchro only {#the-extra-deck}

Synchro and Fusion Monsters do not go in your Main Deck — they live in your separate **Extra Deck**. In Edison, the Extra Deck contains **only Fusion Monsters and Synchro Monsters**. The later Extra-Deck types — **Xyz** (Rank), **Pendulum**, and **Link** — were introduced after this era and are **not** part of Edison. For deck sizes and limits, see the [deck-building primer](/learn/rules/primer-deck-building).
