**TL;DR:** A legal deck is a **Main Deck of 40–60**, an **Extra Deck of 0–15** (Fusion and Synchro only), and a **Side Deck of 0–15**. You may run at most **3 copies** of any card by name — unless the **March 2010 banlist** marks it **Forbidden (0)**, **Limited (1)**, or **Semi-Limited (2)**.

## Main Deck {#main-deck}

Your Main Deck must be **40 to 60 cards**. It holds your Monster, Spell, and Trap Cards — including Ritual Monsters, which live in the Main Deck. Most players stay near the **40-card minimum** so they draw their best cards more consistently.

## Extra Deck {#extra-deck}

Your Extra Deck may hold **0 to 15 cards**, and in this era it contains **only Fusion Monsters and Synchro Monsters**. Extra Deck cards do **not** count toward the 40-card Main Deck minimum. (For how these monsters reach the field, see the [summoning primer](/learn/rules/primer-summoning).)

## Side Deck {#side-deck}

Your Side Deck may hold **0 to 15 cards**. It is a separate set of cards used to adjust your deck **between duels in a match**: after a duel you may swap cards one-for-one between your Side Deck and your Main Deck and/or Extra Deck. The Side Deck's **size is fixed across the match** — after swapping, it must contain **exactly the same number of cards** as before. You show your opponent the count (not the contents).

## Copy limits {#copy-limits}

You may include at most **3 copies of any one card by name**, counted **across your Main Deck, Extra Deck, and Side Deck combined**. Cards with the same name are treated as the same card, so three copies is the cap even if the artwork differs.

## The banlist {#the-banlist}

Some cards are restricted further by the **banlist** (the Forbidden & Limited List). There are three levels:

- **Forbidden** — **0** copies allowed; the card cannot be used at all.
- **Limited** — at most **1** copy.
- **Semi-Limited** — at most **2** copies.

Edison format uses the **March 2010 TCG Forbidden & Limited List**. That specific list is a defining part of the format — it is what keeps the power level frozen to early 2010. The app's deck builder enforces these limits for you and flags anything illegal.
