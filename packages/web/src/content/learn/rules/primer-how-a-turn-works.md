# How a Turn Works {#top}

> **PLACEHOLDER:** This page will be authored by Track C with verified content sourced from edisonformat.com once the in-app docs surface (Track B4) ships.

## Who Goes First {#who-goes-first}

Before the duel begins, both players roll a die (or flip a coin). The winner **chooses** whether to go first or second. Going first has the advantage of setting up your board; going second lets you see what you're up against.

## Turn-1 Battle Phase Restriction {#no-turn-1-battle-phase}

The player who goes **first** cannot conduct a Battle Phase on their first turn. This is a core Edison rule — you cannot attack on turn 1.

## The Six Phases {#six-phases}

Each turn proceeds through these phases in order:

1. **Draw Phase (DP)** — Draw one card from your Deck.
2. **Standby Phase (SP)** — Mandatory effects that trigger "during the Standby Phase" activate here.
3. **Main Phase 1 (MP1)** — Normal Summon, Set, activate Spells/Traps, use effects.
4. **Battle Phase (BP)** — Declare attacks. Skipped on the first player's first turn.
5. **Main Phase 2 (MP2)** — Like MP1; you may Normal Summon again only if you didn't in MP1.
6. **End Phase (EP)** — Discard to the hand limit (6 cards) and resolve end-of-turn effects.

## Priority and the Turn Player {#turn-player-priority}

The turn player acts first in each phase. See **Rule #6 (Ignition Effect Priority)** for the specific rules about when each player can activate effects.
