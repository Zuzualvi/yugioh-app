**TL;DR:** In Edison every Union monster carries the old Condition — a monster can be equipped with only one Union at a time — and only six specific Unions can destroy themselves in place of the monster they equip.

Union monsters had their printed **Conditions** removed in 2016. Edison predates that change, so those Conditions are still live, and they apply to **all** Union monsters in the format — not just the ones whose modern text mentions them.

## One Union per monster {#one-union-per-monster}

A monster can be equipped with **only one Union monster at a time.** This Condition applies to every Union in Edison. Trying to equip a second Union to a monster that already has one equipped is **illegal** — the game will not let you do it.

**Example —** You equip **Machina Gearframe** to a monster. You cannot then equip **Machina Peacekeeper** (a second Union) to that same monster while Gearframe is still equipped — the one-Union limit blocks it. You would have to equip Peacekeeper to a different monster instead.

## Which Unions can "destroy this instead" {#which-unions-protect}

The replacement effect **"If the equipped monster would be destroyed, destroy this card instead"** is present on **only these six** Union monsters in Edison:

- **Armored Cybern**
- **Oilman**
- **Heavy Mech Support Platform**
- **Spirit of the Six Samurai**
- **Machina Gearframe**
- **Machina Peacekeeper**

**Example —** Your monster equipped with **Machina Gearframe** is targeted for destruction. Because Gearframe is on the list, **Gearframe is destroyed instead** and the equipped monster survives (once — the Union is used up).

## Unlisted Unions do NOT protect {#unlisted-unions-dont-protect}

Any Union monster **not** on the list above does **not** protect the monster it equips. When the equipped monster would be destroyed, it is **destroyed normally** — there is no "destroy this instead" substitution.

**Example —** You equip a non-listed Union such as **Y-Dragon Head** or **Z-Metal Tank** to a monster and that monster is targeted for destruction. The equipped monster is **destroyed** — the Union does nothing to save it, because that clause simply is not part of these cards in Edison.

## Related cards {#related-cards}

The archetype rep for this rule is **Machina Gearframe** (and **Machina Peacekeeper**), whose one-Union-per-monster Condition governs every Union. The six "destroy this instead" Unions are **Armored Cybern**, **Oilman**, **Heavy Mech Support Platform**, **Spirit of the Six Samurai**, **Machina Gearframe**, and **Machina Peacekeeper**; classic non-listed Unions such as **Y-Dragon Head** and **Z-Metal Tank** provide no destruction protection in Edison.
