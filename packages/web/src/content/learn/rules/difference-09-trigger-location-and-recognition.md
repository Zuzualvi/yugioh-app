**TL;DR:** A trigger condition can be recognized as met in the middle of a chain, and a Trigger Effect can activate from outside its trigger location — including from the Deck or Extra Deck — unless the card requires it to stay there.

Edison recognizes triggers more loosely than the modern game. Conditions can be noticed mid-chain, and Trigger Effects don't necessarily have to still be where they triggered in order to activate.

## Triggers can be recognized mid-chain {#mid-chain-recognition}

A trigger condition can be **recognized as met in the middle of a chain.** If something happens during the resolution of a chain that satisfies a card's trigger condition, that trigger is recognized and can activate — the modern format generally does not allow this kind of mid-chain recognition.

## Trigger Effects activate from wherever they end up {#triggers-from-anywhere}

A Trigger Effect **activates from outside its trigger location** unless the card specifically requires it to remain there. In other words, the card doesn't have to still be in the place where it triggered — it can have moved (even to the hand) and still activate.

**Example (Dandylion from the Graveyard, even after leaving it) —** You **discard Dandylion** as a **cost** for **Monster Reincarnation** and use Monster Reincarnation to add that same Dandylion **back to your hand.** Dandylion's "Special Summon 2 Fluff Tokens" effect **still triggers and activates from the Graveyard** — even though Dandylion is now sitting in your hand — because it was sent to the Graveyard, which is what its trigger cares about.

## From the Deck and the Extra Deck {#deck-and-extra-deck}

This even extends to cards that end up in the **Deck** or **Extra Deck** — with a key contrast between the two.

- **From the Extra Deck (does activate):** a face-up **Elemental HERO Absolute Zero** returned to the **Extra Deck** from the field (for example by **Phoenix Wing Wind Blast**) **still triggers** its "destroy all monsters your opponent controls" leave-the-field effect — it activates from the Extra Deck.
- **From the Main Deck (does not activate):** **Earthbound Immortal Aslla Piscu** returned to the **Deck** from the field (again by Phoenix Wing Wind Blast) **does not** activate its destroy-and-burn leave-the-field effect. Shuffled into the Main Deck, it loses the trigger.

**Example (mid-chain, from the Deck) —** **Necroface** Normal Summoned with **Future Visions** active can, on resolving, return to the Deck along with the banished cards; because it was **banished by Future Visions**, its second effect (banish the top cards of both Decks) is recognized and **starts a new chain from the Deck** — a trigger activating from within the Deck itself.

## Related cards {#related-cards}

The clean in-format demonstrations are **Dandylion** discarded as a cost for **Monster Reincarnation** (triggers from the Graveyard even after being added back to the hand) and **Elemental HERO Absolute Zero** bounced to the Extra Deck by **Phoenix Wing Wind Blast** (still triggers). The contrasts that teach the boundary are **Earthbound Immortal Aslla Piscu** returned to the Main Deck (does not trigger) and **Necroface** under **Future Visions** (triggers from within the Deck).
