**TL;DR:** After a Summon that did not start a chain, the turn player may activate an Ignition Effect as Chain Link 1 before the opponent can respond.

This is the famous Edison "priority" rule. It changes when you can slip in a monster's Ignition Effect after a Summon — and it is one of the biggest differences from the modern game, where you cannot do this at all.

## After a Summon that did NOT start a chain {#summon-no-chain}

Most Normal and Special Summons do not start a chain by themselves. When a Summon does **not** start a chain, the **turn player gets priority** — the right to activate an **Ignition Effect as Chain Link 1**, in the Summon-response window, **before the opponent may respond.** In practice this lets you use a monster's effect before your opponent can answer the Summon itself with a Trap.

This priority covers Ignition Effects wherever they live:

- **Monster-Zone Ignition Effects.** After Special Summoning **Chaos Sorcerer**, you may banish a monster with its effect **before** the opponent can activate **Bottomless Trap Hole** on the Summon. After Ritual Summoning **Demise, King of Armageddon**, you keep priority for Demise's Ignition before the opponent's **Torrential Tribute.**
- **Graveyard Ignition Effects.** Discard **Destiny Hero - Malicious** to Special Summon **Dark Grepher**, then activate Malicious's "banish self" Ignition from the Graveyard **before** the opponent can respond with **D.D. Crow.**

**Example —** You Special Summon **Chaos Sorcerer**. The Summon did not start a chain, so you have priority: you activate Chaos Sorcerer's banish Ignition as Chain Link 1. Because a chain has now started with your effect, your opponent's **Bottomless Trap Hole** can only respond to the chain — it has lost the clean "when a monster is Summoned" window.

## When the Summon DOES start a chain {#summon-starts-a-chain}

You only get this fresh Chain Link 1 when the Summon **did not** start a chain. If a chain is **already** starting as the Summon happens, you do **not** get to insert an Ignition as a new Chain Link 1 — you can only build onto the existing chain with fast effects (if any). Three cases to know:

- **The opponent's trigger starts the chain.** If your Special Summon triggers your opponent's effect — for example an opponent's face-up **Black Garden** reacting to the Summon — that trigger starts the chain. You may then only add fast effects onto that chain; you **cannot** insert a monster's Ignition as a fresh Chain Link 1.
- **The summoning material's own trigger starts the chain.** If a material's trigger fires from being used — for example **Sangan** sent to the Graveyard as Synchro Material — that trigger starts the chain, and you **cannot** activate the newly Synchro-Summoned monster's Ignition (such as **Brionac, Dragon of the Ice Barrier**) in that window.
- **A Normal Summon of a monster that has a Trigger Effect.** On Normal Summoning a monster with its own Trigger Effect (such as **Armageddon Knight**), you may activate **that** Trigger Effect or pass — but you have **no** priority to activate any _other_ card or effect as Chain Link 1. Your opponent may then respond (for example with **Torrential Tribute**).

> **⚠ Known table-difference —** When your Special Summon triggers your OPPONENT'S mandatory effect (such as an opponent's face-up **Black Garden**), that trigger starts the chain, so at a live table you may only add fast effects onto it — you do NOT get to insert a monster's Ignition Effect as a fresh Chain Link 1. The app may still offer your ignition as a fresh Chain Link 1 in this exact spot. If it does, that is a known difference: at the table you have no fresh ignition priority once the opponent's trigger has opened the chain.

## Priority is a right, whether or not you declare it {#priority-is-a-right}

Ignition Effect Priority is a **right that exists whether or not you say the word "priority."** It sits on top of the normal fast-effect-timing rules — you are not required to announce it for it to apply, and passing it lets your opponent act. Knowing you _have_ it is what matters: after a chain-less Summon, the decision to use a monster's Ignition before the opponent responds is yours.

## Related cards {#related-cards}

The headline priority plays use **Chaos Sorcerer** (Monster-Zone Ignition before **Bottomless Trap Hole**), **Demise, King of Armageddon** (before **Torrential Tribute**), and **Destiny Hero - Malicious** (Graveyard Ignition before **D.D. Crow**). The "no fresh Chain Link 1" cases involve **Black Garden**, **Sangan** as Synchro Material for **Brionac, Dragon of the Ice Barrier**, and **Armageddon Knight**. Note that several of these monsters are **not** once-per-turn in Edison — see the card reference for **Brionac**, **Sangan**, and **Rescue Cat**.
