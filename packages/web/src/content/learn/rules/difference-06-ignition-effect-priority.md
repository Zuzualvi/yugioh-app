# Rule #6 — Ignition Effect Priority {#top}

> **PLACEHOLDER:** This page will be authored by Track C with verified content sourced from edisonformat.com once the in-app docs surface (Track B4) ships. The structure below demonstrates the intended article format.

**TL;DR:** On your turn, after a Summon that didn't start a chain, YOU may activate an Ignition Effect as Chain Link 1 before your opponent can respond (e.g. before Bottomless Trap Hole or Torrential Tribute).

## When the Summon Does NOT Start a Chain {#summon-no-chain}

Most Normal Summons and Special Summons do not start a chain by themselves. When this happens:

1. The turn player (you) gets **priority** — the right to activate an Ignition Effect as Chain Link 1.
2. You may use this to activate a monster's Ignition Effect immediately after the summon.
3. Only after you **pass priority** (or activate an effect) can your opponent respond with a Trap card like Bottomless Trap Hole.

**Example:** You Normal Summon Chaos Sorcerer. It does not start a chain. You invoke priority and banish a monster from your opponent's Graveyard with Chaos Sorcerer's Ignition Effect. Your opponent cannot activate Bottomless Trap Hole to respond to the Summon because the chain has already started.

## When the Summon DOES Start a Chain {#summon-starts-a-chain}

Some monsters have Continuous, Trigger, or Flip Effects that activate automatically when Summoned, putting them on the chain. In this case:

1. No priority window exists — the chain is already open.
2. Your opponent may respond to that chain link normally.
3. The turn player does not get a separate priority window for an Ignition Effect after the chain resolves.

## Related Cards {#related-cards}

Cards frequently involved in priority questions: **Chaos Sorcerer**, **Sangan** (Trigger, not Ignition), **Brionac, Dragon of the Ice Barrier** (Ignition).
